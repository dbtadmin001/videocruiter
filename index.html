The Agency's own advice
  • Treat it like a normal face-to-face interview.
  • Find uninterrupted time — the whole thing runs in one sitting.
  • Have a pen and paper to take notes.
  • Speak in a conversational voice; dress professionally.
  • Be confident, smile when appropriate, mind gestures and posture.

Assessors are told to weigh the content of your answers first, then structure, focus and clarity, then the depth of your examples. Body language matters but is explicitly "not essential".
